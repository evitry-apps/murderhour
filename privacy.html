Privacy policy

Your data in Murder Hour

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Murder Hour is made by Eloi Vitry, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here. In short: no account, no e-mail address, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

What we collect

  • A random player identifier. When you open the app or an invitation link, our server creates an anonymous session so that you come back as the same player. It is not linked to your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • The first name you type, shown to the other players of your game.
  • What you do in a game: your character, the clues and items you share or trade, your votes and your accusation. This is needed to run the game and show the scores.
  • Technical logs: our web server records IP addresses and browser or device information for security and troubleshooting. These logs are deleted after 14 days.

What we do not collect

No e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where the data is stored

On our own server, rented from Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany (European Union), which hosts it on our behalf. No other company receives your data.

How long we keep it

Games are kept so that players can reopen them and see the scores. You can ask us at any time to delete a game and the data of its players.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data or for its deletion, by writing to the address on the support page with your game code. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Murder Hour is a party game for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date.
