Murder Hour

The one-hour murder mystery. No prep, nothing to print, and everyone plays, the host too.

The host uses the iPhone app. Friends join from their phone's browser with a link or a QR code: no download, no account. The app deals each player a secret character, runs three acts of clues, votes and trades, then reveals who did it.

4 to 6 players · about one hour · first mystery included
