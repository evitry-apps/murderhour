How do I host a game?

Install Murder Hour on your iPhone, tap Host a game, enter your first name, then share the invitation link or show the QR code. When everyone is in, tap Deal the roles. Everyone reads their character, then you start the game. As the host you play too: you do not know the solution.

Do my friends need the app?

No. Guests play in their phone's browser (iPhone or Android) from the link or the QR code. Only the host needs the iPhone app.

How many players?

The included mystery, The Last Signing, plays with 4 to 6 people in the same room and lasts about an hour.

Someone cancelled or left during the game

Deal the roles to whoever is there: the mystery stays solvable for every head count. If a player leaves during the game, the host can remove them in Host tools; the app then plays their character and passes their clues on.

Can I try it alone first?

Yes: on the home screen, tap Try it alone first. You play the whole story with three simulated players.
